Orange 1.0.2 adds a startup guard for Maya crashing on every launch after Orange was installed (reported on macOS). Orange now records how far it got while starting. If Maya closed during the previous start, Orange does not start automatically, Maya opens normally, and the Orange menu shows the step that did not finish with a **Start now** item. The step is also included in the "Report an issue" diagnostics.

The update check now reads the system proxy settings on the main thread. On macOS, Python looks them up through SystemConfiguration, and doing that from a background thread inside Maya could crash it.

F can now fill a hole one face at a time, like in Blender. Select a single vertex on the border of a hole and press F: Orange adds one face at that corner and selects the next vertex, so pressing F again fills the next face without creating n-gons. Triangle and quad holes are closed completely. With a connected chain of border edges selected, F makes one face from those vertices instead of closing the whole hole.
